## 2. Business Overview

Katahdin Trail & Paddle is a locally operated outdoor recreation company serving visitors to the Katahdin and Millinocket region of Maine.

The company provides recreational equipment rentals and guided outdoor experiences designed to help families, tourists, beginners, and outdoor enthusiasts enjoy Maine's lakes, rivers, and trails safely and confidently.

The business focuses primarily on accessible outdoor experiences rather than highly technical or extreme adventures.

Customers may rent equipment for independent recreation or participate in guided activities led by experienced staff.

Safety, preparation, responsible recreation, and respect for Maine's natural environment are central to the company.

# 19. Frequently Asked Questions

The following questions and answers are approved for website use.

---

### Do I need previous paddling experience?

No.

Several guided trips are specifically designed for beginners.

Customers renting equipment independently should choose activities appropriate for their ability and current conditions.

---

### Are life jackets included with watercraft rentals?

Required basic safety equipment is provided with watercraft rentals.

Customers should follow all instructions regarding personal flotation devices.

---

### Can children participate in guided trips?

Yes, depending on the trip.

Each guided trip lists a recommended minimum age.

Final participation may also depend on current conditions and individual ability.

---

### Should I reserve rental equipment in advance?

Yes.

Advance reservations are strongly recommended during:

* weekends,
* holidays,
* summer,
* and periods of high visitor activity.

---

### What happens if the weather is bad?

Outdoor activities may be delayed, modified, rescheduled, or canceled if conditions are unsafe.

Safety takes priority over maintaining the original schedule.

---

### What should I wear?

Customers should dress for current and expected conditions.

Quick-drying clothing is recommended for paddling.

Appropriate trail footwear is recommended for hiking.

---

### Can beginners rent equipment without joining a guided trip?

Yes, depending on the equipment and current conditions.

Staff may provide basic equipment information, but rental service should not be presented as formal instruction.

Customers are responsible for choosing activities appropriate for their ability.
