About Us

Do you want to experience Maine's lakes, rivers, and trails?

Katahdin Trail & Paddle will make your adventures unforgettable. With your safety our top priority, we aim to create an environment where anyone, whether a beginner, a tourist, or families, can hike, bike, kayak, and do other outdoor activities. We can also provide a guide for these activities to make your journey comfortable. Your journey matters most to us.
